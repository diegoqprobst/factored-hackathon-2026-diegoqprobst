Hi, I'm Diego. This is a dispute agent for LATAM Bank that knows when not to act. It handles card disputes end to end in Spanish and Portuguese: it verifies who you are, finds the exact charge, files the dispute, and when policy says a person should decide, it hands off with everything already verified. On 230 held-out conversations it safely resolved every in-scope dispute, 104 out of 104, against 86.5 percent for a rules-only baseline, it made every required handoff, it had no unsafe outcome, and the LLM costs about six hundredths of a cent per resolved case.
