Why disputes? We picked the workflow from the data, not from a guess. Complaint contacts are fixed on first contact only 44 percent of the time, versus 92 for transactional ones, and 63 percent need a follow-up. Unrecognized charges and undue fees are about 40 percent of formal complaints. Disputes also have the one trustworthy ground truth in this dataset: transactions tie cleanly to products and customers, so the agent can verify the exact charge. And the data shows why customer isolation must live in code: not one complaint product belongs to the person who complained.
